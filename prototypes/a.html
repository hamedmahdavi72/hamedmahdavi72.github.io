About

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science and Engineering at Pennsylvania State University, advised by Prof. Vasant Honavar, graduating in November 2026. My research is on reasoning in large language models. I build agentic workflows and evaluation harnesses that find where models fail, generate targeted synthetic data to fix those failures, and feed it back through SFT, RL, and pretraining.

Most recently I was a Research Scientist Intern at Prelude in New York, training reward models for creative judgment. Before that I collaborated with Google's Gemini Data team on the Gemini IMO 2025 project, and my work on LLMs for mathematics is supported by an unrestricted research gift from Google.

I am on the job market for research scientist roles starting late 2026, with a focus on LLM post-training, reasoning, and data.
